Data sources & methodology

Market Daily generates fictional equities and index observations for each completed NYSE session. The archive retains 30 sessions. Each index chart shows simulated daily closes through the selected date.

Universe and ranking rules

The intended universe covers active U.S. dollar common shares, common REITs and ADRs primarily listed on Nasdaq, NYSE or NYSE American. Funds, preferreds, warrants, units, rights and OTC listings are excluded. Distinct traded share classes retain their own listings. The overall ranking has no price floor and may include volatile microcaps.

Each return compares the regular close with the previous trading session’s split-comparable close. The large-cap ranking evaluates all eligible companies with a verified market capitalization of at least $10 billion and a cap observation no more than seven calendar days old. Ties sort by ticker.

Provider coverage and display rights

The reviewed free feeds did not establish public redistribution rights and complete coverage of eligible equities. Alpha Vantage’s 20 movers per side cannot establish the largest moves among all large caps. All four lists and all three indices here use synthetic observations.
